Download these two files and place them in this folder (lib):

1. https://repo1.maven.org/maven2/org/xerial/sqlite-jdbc/3.46.1.3/sqlite-jdbc-3.46.1.3.jar
2. https://repo1.maven.org/maven2/org/slf4j/slf4j-api/1.7.36/slf4j-api-1.7.36.jar

(sqlite-jdbc is the database driver. slf4j-api is a small library the driver uses.
A harmless "SLF4J: no providers / NOP logger" message at start-up is normal.)
